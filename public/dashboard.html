Connect Qwen account

Sign in with your Qwen account — the same login as chat.qwen.ai. Your password is SHA-256 hashed in the browser and never stored; only the session token is kept.

  1. Sign in at chat.qwen.ai in your browser.
  2. Copy your token: open DevTools (F12) → Console → run localStorage.getItem("token") and copy the value (no quotes).
  3. Paste it below — it's validated live before anything is saved.

Connect DeepSeek account

Sign in with your DeepSeek account — the same login as chat.deepseek.com. Your password is SHA-256 hashed in the browser and never stored; only the session token is kept. Note: each sign-in invalidates the previous session for that account.

DeepSeek sent a verification code to your email — enter it to finish signing in.

  1. Sign in at chat.deepseek.com in your browser.
  2. Copy your token: open DevTools (F12) → Console → run JSON.parse(localStorage.getItem("userToken")).value and copy the value (no quotes).
  3. Paste it below — it's validated live before anything is saved.

Connect Z.ai account

  1. Sign in at chat.z.ai in your browser — solve the captcha there (one time).
  2. Copy your token: open DevTools (F12) → Application → Local Storage → https://chat.z.ai → copy the token value.
  3. Paste it below — it's validated live before anything is saved.
